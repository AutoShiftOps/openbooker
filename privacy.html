Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2026

This policy explains what data openbooker collects and how it's used. openbooker is built for independent service businesses (the "tenant") to manage their own appointments and client relationships.

1. Two kinds of data

Your account data — your email address, business profile (name, hours, branding), and subscription/billing status — is used to operate your account and is controlled by openbooker as described below.

Your clients' data — names, phone numbers, addresses, appointment history, and marketing-consent flags you enter about the people you serve — belongs to you, the tenant. openbooker stores and processes this data on your behalf, as a processor, not as its own data. You are responsible for having a lawful basis to collect and retain it.

2. What we store

  • Account: email, authentication credentials (handled by Firebase Authentication), subscription status.
  • Business profile: name, branding, hours, currency, timezone, and any logo image you upload.
  • Appointments, clients, inventory, and services you enter.
  • Push notification tokens, only if you enable automatic reminders.

3. Where it's stored

Data is stored using Google Firebase (Firestore, Authentication, Cloud Messaging) and served via Firebase Hosting. Subscription billing is handled by Stripe; openbooker does not store your payment card details directly.

4. Data isolation

Each tenant's data is isolated to their own account and is not visible to other tenants. Server-side rules enforce this at the database level.

5. Data retention & deletion

Your data is retained for as long as your account is active. You can request deletion of your account and associated data by contacting support.

6. Third parties

We use Google Firebase (infrastructure) and Stripe (billing) as sub-processors. We don't sell your data or your clients' data to third parties.

7. Contact

Questions about this policy, or requests to access or delete data, can be sent to the support contact listed in the app.
